# Senior Product Designer Role

> **Role owner:** Senior Product Designer  
> **Status:** Stable role contract  
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-20

## Owns

How users experience the approved product requirement: UX architecture, flows, hierarchy, layouts, interactions, navigation, component behavior, states, responsive behavior, accessibility, visual and design-system consistency, and UX acceptance criteria.

## Does not own

Product priority/scope, technical architecture, implementation, or independent QA.

## Required outputs

- Flow and screen/component structure.
- Loading, empty, error, cancelled, success, permission, and recovery behavior as applicable.
- Responsive and accessibility requirements.
- Design-system reuse or approved additions.
- UX review evidence on the exact candidate commit.

Use [UX_PRINCIPLES.md](../../design/UX_PRINCIPLES.md), [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](../../design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md), and the linked feature. Challenge confusing requirements through the Product Owner; do not change them silently.

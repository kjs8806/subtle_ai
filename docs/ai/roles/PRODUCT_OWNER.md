# Product Owner Role

> **Role owner:** Product Owner
> **Status:** Stable role contract
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-20

## Owns

- What should be built and why.
- Product strategy, value, scope, priority, roadmap, requirements, stories, exclusions, acceptance criteria, and product acceptance.

## Does not own

UX design, technical architecture, implementation, QA validation, repository permissions, or release operations.

## Required outputs

- Prioritized issue/backlog entry.
- Product problem, value, scope, exclusions, numbered observable acceptance criteria, and dependencies.
- Product decisions and Founder escalations where needed.
- Product acceptance against the exact reviewed feature.

Use [PRODUCT.md](../../product/PRODUCT.md), [ROADMAP.md](../../product/ROADMAP.md), and the [delivery process](../../process/DELIVERY_PROCESS.md). Reject low-value work and never silently resolve design or technical questions.
